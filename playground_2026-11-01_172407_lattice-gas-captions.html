Walker models and collisions

What happens when two walkers meet depends on what a walker is. The playground offers two walker models and two collision behaviours; they are part of the rule configuration, next to the rules themselves.

walker modela walker iswhen two walkers want the same place
edgethe edge {w,s}: a position, nothing else nothing happens. Any number of walkers may share a site and they pass through each other; the chain rules move every walker independently. This is the model of the Brownian imitation: point particles that do not interact.
hyperedgethe hyperedge {w,s,s'}: a position and a heading, the direction label k (+x, −x, +y, −y) of the edge from s to s' the exclusion principle: at most one walker per channel (s, k), so at most four walkers per site and never two with the same heading. A rule that would put two walkers into one channel is blocked for the loser: it keeps its channel (ties are drawn at random, a channel that is taken in the current state is never entered). The HPP lattice gas never blocks; blocked hops are counted in the measured values.

hppthe Collide rule of the HPP lattice gas (Hardy, Pomeau, de Pazzis 1973) runs first in every step: {w1,s,a}, {w2,s,b}, {s,c}, {s,d} → {w1,s,c}, {w2,s,d}, {s,c}, {s,d}. Two walkers on one site heading in opposite directions a, b leave along the perpendicular pair c, d, provided those channels are empty and are real edges (not wall self-loops); which walker takes c is drawn at random. Needs hyperedge walkers, because a collision needs a direction to change. The collision sites are ringed in the step-by-step animation. walkers, momentum (a + b = 0 = c + d), energy (every walker keeps the speed a / τ), the exclusion

Rules on hyperedge walkers: a chain rule moves the walker along its chain as it does for edge walkers, and the label of the last hop becomes the new heading (a walker that stays keeps its heading). The built-in rules Stream (move one edge along the heading, keep the label) and Reflect (a walker heading into a wall self-loop turns round, the wall takes the momentum) only exist for hyperedge walkers; ticking Stream and Reflect with hpp collisions is the HPP lattice gas of the gas report. The drift and diffusion prediction counts the chain rules only, and holds at low density; blocked hops lower the measured values when the world is crowded.

5. Reproduce

The session as the GUI exports it. Save it as session.json and run python -m imigraph playground-report --request-file session.json --label <name>, or import it in the GUI.

{
  "rule": [
    {
      "name": "Reflect",
      "builtin": "reflect",
      "description": "a walker heading into a wall turns round"
    },
    {
      "name": "Stream",
      "builtin": "stream",
      "description": "free flight: one edge per step along the heading"
    }
  ],
  "walker_model": "hyperedge",
  "collisions": "hpp",
  "heading": "random",
  "size": 9,
  "walls": "reflecting",
  "walkers": 24,
  "placement": "left",
  "steps": 60,
  "seed": 3
}
